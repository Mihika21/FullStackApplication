1. Executive Summary
The HGV GCC Workforce Master Data & Billing Platform is a Phase 1 internal enterprise application intended to replace the current Excel-based workforce and billing administration process. The current model uses multiple workbooks for GCC, NOC, Rebadge, Colocation, Non-GCC Fixed Bid and related workforce populations. Manual maintenance creates data-entry drift, stale totals, blank fields and inconsistent calculations.
The platform will provide a single validated source of truth, controlled reference data, compensation capture, configurable billing rules, CSV bulk upload, finance-ready Excel/PDF reports and role-based access. It will support GCC and NOC salary-based billing, Rebadge and Colocation facility/user-bundle billing, and maintain Non-GCC Fixed Bid master data without calculating its billing.
This PRD expands the attached BRD into product behavior, workflows, acceptance criteria, architecture recommendations and implementation detail. The attached sample PRD is used only as a reference for explanatory depth and structure; its procurement use case is not carried into this product.
Confirmed product decisionsJoining Bonus: three-month amortization. Variable Pay: None / Flat % / Custom Amount, with Flat % applied to monthly Fixed Salary. Technology: a recommended technology stack is included.

7.3 Variable Pay
The BRD's earlier symbolic wording could be circular if Variable Pay were calculated from Salary Cost. This PRD resolves that ambiguity using the confirmed rule: Flat % is applied to monthly fixed salary.
Mode
Calculation
None
Variable Pay Amount = 0
Flat %

Custom Amount
Variable Pay Amount = configured/entered custom amount

7.4 Salary Cost
FormulaSalary Cost (USD) = (Monthly Fixed Salary (INR) ÷ FX Rate) + Variable Pay Amount (USD)

Where custom variable pay is stored in INR, it should be converted consistently using the configured FX rate.
7.5 Joining Bonus — Three-Month Amortization
Joining Bonus is included in billing and amortized over three billing months. The platform applies one-third of the recorded bonus amount to each of the three applicable months and stops the component after month three.
FormulaMonthly Joining Bonus Component = Joining Bonus Amount ÷ 3, applied for three billing months.

7.6 Fully Loaded Cost
FormulaFully Loaded Cost = Salary Cost + Operational Margin Amount + Facility Cost + User Bundle Cost + Shift Allowance + Monthly Joining Bonus ComponentOperational Margin Amount = Salary Cost × Operational Margin %

7.7 Rebadge & Colocation
FormulaFully Loaded Cost = Facility Cost + User Bundle Cost

20. Acceptance Criteria Summary
Authorized users can maintain Phase 1 workforce records without Excel.
Mandatory fields vary correctly by Engagement Type.
Engagement Type and Employment Category remain separate.
Controlled values cannot be silently created through CSV.
CSV supports partial success and row-level errors.
Monthly Shift Allowance Upload supports Employee ID, Billing Month and validated amount.
GCC/NOC billing produces monthly Salary Cost and Fully Loaded Cost using configuration.
Variable Pay supports None, Flat % and Custom Amount.
Flat % is based on monthly Fixed Salary.
Joining Bonus is amortized across three billing months.
Rebadge/Colocation billing contains Facility + User Bundle and no salary.
Non-GCC Fixed Bid has no platform billing calculation.
HR Manager can update Billing Configuration without deployment.
Sensitive configuration changes are auditable.
Reports support required filters and Excel/PDF outputs.
Sensitive PII is protected and masked according to authorization.
At least 2,000 records are supported without material degradation.
Architecture remains suitable for future dashboards and HRMS integration.
